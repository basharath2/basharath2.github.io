Continuing professional development

This free course can support your CPD. Many professional bodies, such as ISC2®, PMI® and PeopleCert, let members self-report relevant self-study. Whether it counts, and how many hours, is decided by your body under its current rules. The course has 1 hour 12 minutes of video. Some bodies, such as ISACA®, accept self-study only with a certificate from the course provider; this free course does not issue one, so check your body's rules before you claim.
