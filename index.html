Questions

Are the kits really free?
Yes. There is no sign-up and we do not ask for your email.
Can we use them inside our organisation?
Yes, within one organisation. Resale and redistribution are not allowed.
Do the kits include the text of the standard?
No. They reference clause numbers and paraphrase. You need your own licensed copy of the standard.
How do I report an error?
Write to hello@brivellis.com. Corrections ship in the next version.
