About brivellis

Complete, free governance kits for teams worldwide.

Brivellis publishes complete, free governance kits, practical insights and self-assessment tools for IT service management, cybersecurity, business resilience and governance.

Our purpose

Make technology progress clearer, more connected, and more human.

We make IT service management, cybersecurity, resilience and governance easier to understand and act on. Everything we publish is free and written for teams worldwide, built on international standards such as ISO/IEC 20000-1, ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 22301, with practical guidance on the regional regulations teams answer to.

What guides us

Principles behind our work.

Premium does not mean distant. Futuristic does not mean vague. Our standard is thoughtful work, clear communication, and accountable delivery.

01

Clarity before activity

We make the outcome, constraints, choices, and measures visible before momentum becomes motion without direction.

02

Candor over hype

We communicate directly, and surface difficult decisions early enough to act on them.

03

Technology without dogma

The right architecture, platform, vendor, or operating model follows the need - never the other way around.

04

Results that endure

We design for adoption, ownership, resilience, and improvement beyond the initial delivery milestone.

What we cover

Four domains we publish on.

IT Service Management

Running IT services well, from ITIL® practices to an ISO/IEC 20000-1 service management system.

Cybersecurity & Compliance

Building and evidencing a security programme that auditors and regulators can follow.

Business Resilience

Keeping critical services running through disruption, from impact analysis to recovery.

Governance & Risk

How organisations decide, prioritise and stay in control as technology changes.
