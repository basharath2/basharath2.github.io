Cybersecurity Awareness Programme Kit

Most organisations run security awareness training. Far fewer can prove it in an audit. This free kit gives you 11 editable files for a planned awareness programme: a policy, a 12-month plan, four module outlines, rules for phishing simulations, a register, a metrics dashboard and a crosswalk. Together they produce the five pieces of evidence that auditors ask for under ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and NIST CSF 2.0. Every file carries a worked example for a fictional organisation. No sign-up.

The five pieces of evidence

Auditors do not assess whether training took place. They assess whether a planned programme exists, whether it reached the right people, and whether anyone checked that it worked.

  1. An approved programme plan (file 02, with the policy, 01)
  2. Content matched to role and risk (file 03)
  3. Attendance and completion records (file 05)
  4. Effectiveness measures (file 06, with the rules in 04)
  5. A review record (file 06, Review Record tab)

Questions before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. It is free to use within one organisation, with no sign-up. You may not resell or redistribute it.

Does it include the training itself?

No. It includes outlines for four modules, with learning outcomes, key points and knowledge-check questions. You build the slides, e-learning or talks from them with your own rules and examples.

Which frameworks does it cover?

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (clauses 7.2 and 7.3, Annex A control 6.3) and NIST CSF 2.0 (PR.AT-01, PR.AT-02, ID.IM-01). The crosswalk shows which file answers which reference.

Do I need to buy the standards?

For ISO/IEC 27001 certification you need a licensed copy; the kit refers to clause numbers and does not reproduce the text. NIST CSF 2.0 is free from NIST.

Will using the kit get us certified?

No. The kit is a starting set of templates. Your organisation is responsible for running the programme, and a certification body decides on certification.

Can I use AI to tailor it?

Yes, with care. The kit includes a prompt pack and six rules. AI drafts; a named person reviews and approves. Never use AI to create attendance records, results or sign-offs.

How we use AI

The templates were drafted with AI assistance, then reviewed, corrected and approved by a person before release. The AI Tailoring Prompt Pack helps you adapt them with your own AI tool: use a business account, keep personal and confidential data out, and have a competent person approve every document. AI never creates evidence; your records come from running the processes.
