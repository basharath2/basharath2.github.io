Fourteen editable templates and worked examples for a service management system aligned with ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 and Amendment 1:2024. Most teams have a first full draft in 2 to 4 weeks.

Questions before you start.

Will this kit get us certified?

No. Certification comes from an accredited certification body after it audits your service management system in operation. The kit gives you the core documents to start from; you still need to run the processes and keep the records they call for.

What is included and what is not?

The kit covers the core documents: scope, policy, SMS plan, SLA, management review and nonconformity, plus a workbook with a readiness checklist, internal audit checklist, service catalogue, KPI register and crosswalk. Every template has a worked example. Operational process documents for clause 8 (incident, change, problem, request, release) are not included.

Do I need a copy of the standard?

Yes. The kit references clause numbers only and does not reproduce text from the standard. Buy a licensed copy of ISO/IEC 20000-1:2018 and Amendment 1:2024 from ISO or your national standards body.

Does it work with ITIL?

Yes. The workbook includes a crosswalk from ISO/IEC 20000-1 clauses to ITIL® practices and to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls, so you can avoid duplicate work.

Can I use AI to tailor the documents?

Yes. The AI Tailoring Prompt Pack has prompts for ChatGPT, Claude and Microsoft Copilot. Use a business AI account, do not paste personal or confidential data, and have a competent person review every document before approval.

Can consultants use the kit with clients?

The free licence covers internal use within one organisation. Each client organisation should download its own copy. Redistribution and resale are not allowed.

Do you collect my details?

No. There is no sign-up. The download is served by GitHub, and Brivellis sees only an anonymous total download count.

How do I check my work?

Score your scope statement, policy and one SLA with the free capstone rubric (aim for 16 out of 20). It is a self-assessment; nothing is uploaded. Open the capstone rubric (PDF).

Is there a course for this kit?

Yes. It is free on YouTube: 26 short lessons across 8 modules (about 85 minutes in total) that walk through the kit template by template. Start the free course.

How we use AI

The templates were drafted with AI assistance, then reviewed, corrected and approved by a person before release. The AI Tailoring Prompt Pack helps you adapt them with your own AI tool: use a business account, keep personal and confidential data out, and have a competent person approve every document. AI never creates evidence; your records come from running the processes.
