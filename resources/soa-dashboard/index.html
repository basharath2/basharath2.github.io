SoA dashboard for ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Open your Statement of Applicability (SoA), from the ISMS Starter Kit or your own template, and see which controls still need an owner, evidence or a decision, in a suggested order. Your file is read in this browser and never uploaded.

File 05 of the ISMS Starter Kit or your own Statement of Applicability, as .xlsx or CSV, up to 5 MB. Read in this browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.

Download the kit →

How to use it

Four steps. Not sure where to start? Try the Falcon Ridge example first.

  1. Step 1

    Open your file

    Choose your Statement of Applicability as .xlsx or CSV (up to 5 MB), or drop it on the dashed box. It is read in this browser and never uploaded.

  2. Step 2

    Check the columns

    If your template uses its own column names, the tool shows which columns it used. If it cannot tell, pick three: control number, applicable and status. Use Change columns to correct a guess.

  3. Step 3

    Read the dashboard

    See how many applicable controls are implemented, partial, planned or not started, overall and by theme.

  4. Step 4

    Work the list

    Fix first lists the gaps to close first, such as a missing reason for an exclusion or a control marked in place with no evidence. Fix next is good practice, such as an owner for each control. Print it or download the list.
